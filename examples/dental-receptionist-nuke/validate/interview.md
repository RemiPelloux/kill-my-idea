# Interview script

Who: Owner-dentists and office managers of independent 1-3 location practices on Open Dental

Ask about what they did, not what they would do. Don't pitch until the last minute.

1. Walk me through the last time the phones rang and nobody could pick up. What happened?
2. What happens today to a call that comes in at lunch or after 5 pm?
3. When did you last find out a new patient called and never got through? How did you find out?
4. What have you tried for missed calls so far: voicemail, an answering service, Weave, extra staff?
5. What do you pay today for phones and call coverage, and on what contract?
6. Last time you switched a phone or software vendor, who decided and how long did it take?
7. What went wrong the last time a tool booked or handled patients for you?
8. Which practice-management system do you use, and who manages it?

## Red flags

- They can't remember a recent missed call
- They already use Weave's AI receptionist and are happy with it
- They are not on Open Dental and won't switch
- Phone decisions sit with a DSO head office
