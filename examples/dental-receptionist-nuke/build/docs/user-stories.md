# User stories: LunchLine

Generated by kill-my-idea:build on 2026-11-12 from the PIVOT 56/100 verdict, building fix: An Open Dental-native, HIPAA-covered AI receptionist for independent practices, $199/month with no contract, sold by missed-call audits.

## Personas

- **The swamped office manager**: Office manager at an independent 1-3 location practice. Pain: Phones ring during check-ins and lunch; callers can't get through
  > "create frustrated callers who WANT to become new patients, but cannot successfully get through to make an appointment." ([source](https://www.dentaltown.com/channel/post/9320/when-your-successful-marketing-overwhelms-your-front-desk))
- **The contract-burned owner**: Owner-dentist locked into phone or answering contracts. Pain: Long contracts and tools that don't work with Open Dental
  > "not very compatible with Open Dental, always gives us issues" ([source](https://www.capterra.com/p/141842/Weave/reviews/?page=2))

## Must have

### US-01 · 2 pts · sprint 0

As **The contract-burned owner**, I want to receive my missed-call audit result by email, so that I see how many calls my office misses before I buy anything.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] 40 practices are called at lunch or after 5 pm and each result is logged in tracker.csv
- [ ] Each owner email names the time of the test call and its outcome

### US-02 · 3 pts · sprint 0

As **The contract-burned owner**, I want a demo number that answers as a sample practice, so that I hear what my patients would hear.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] The demo line answers within 2 rings
- [ ] 5 of 5 test calls end with a correct booking request

### US-03 · 5 pts · sprint 1

As **The swamped office manager**, I want the AI to offer real open slots from Open Dental, so that patients only get times we can honour.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] Slots come from the clinic's Open Dental schedule, not a static list
- [ ] No slot overlapping an existing appointment is ever offered in 50 test calls

### US-04 · 8 pts · sprint 2

As **The swamped office manager**, I want the AI to write the booked appointment into Open Dental, so that nobody re-types bookings from voicemail.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] A booked call creates the appointment in Open Dental with patient name and phone
- [ ] 95% of 100 test bookings are correct with no manual fix

### US-05 · 3 pts · sprint 2

As **The swamped office manager**, I want callers who ask for a person to be handed to the front desk or a call-back list, so that no patient is stuck with a robot.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] Saying 'a person' transfers during open hours
- [ ] After hours, a call-back entry with the caller's number is created

### US-06 · 3 pts · sprint 1

As **The contract-burned owner**, I want every call to play recording and AI disclosure, under a signed BAA, so that my practice stays HIPAA and state-law compliant.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] The disclosure plays in the first 10 seconds of every call
- [ ] Signed BAAs with Retell and AWS are stored in the compliance folder

### US-07 · 2 pts · sprint 3

As **The contract-burned owner**, I want to pay $199 a month and cancel by email, so that I'm never locked in.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] Stripe charges $199 monthly with no setup fee
- [ ] A cancel request by email stops the next charge within 1 business day


## Should have

### US-08 · 3 pts · sprint 3

As **The swamped office manager**, I want a weekly summary of calls answered and booked, so that I can see the value.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] The email lists calls answered, booked and handed off for the week
- [ ] Numbers match the call log for 2 sample weeks
