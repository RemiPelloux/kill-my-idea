| Risk | L | I | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|

## 10. Rescue paths, 7-day test and kill criteria

| # | Change | Score after | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open Dental-native and no-contract: write-back booking, done-for-you setup, month-to-month, sold by cold email to Open Dental practices | 63/100 (estimate) | hard |
| 2 | White-label to dental marketing agencies or IT providers, who resell at $199+ | 60/100 (estimate) | hard |
| 3 | Local metro: phone and walk-in outreach to 200 independent dentists, concierge setup | 58/100 (estimate) | doable |

- **Day 1:** One-page site: 'We answer the calls your front desk misses, $199/month', with a booking link and a demo number → verify: Page and demo line live, under $30 spent
- **Day 2:** Call 40 independent practices at lunch and after 5 pm; log which go unanswered → verify: 10 or more of 40 calls unanswered
- **Day 3:** Build a Retell demo agent that answers as a sample practice and takes a booking request → verify: 5 test calls booked correctly
- **Day 4:** Email 150 owners, including those you called: 'I called your office at 12:40 and nobody answered' → verify: 6 or more replies
- **Day 6:** Run demos; offer a 30-day paid pilot at $199 → verify: 3 or more demos held
- **Day 7:** Count pilots and objections (HIPAA, PMS, voice quality) → verify: 2 or more paid pilots or signed letters of intent

**Stop if:** Fewer than 8 of 40 test calls to clinics go unanswered by day 2 -> the pain is weak, stop; Fewer than 3 demo bookings from 150 cold emails by day 7 -> stop; 0 paid pilots ($199 first month) from 5 demos by day 10 -> stop
