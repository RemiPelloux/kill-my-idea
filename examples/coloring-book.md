## What happened

This is the idea that created kill-my-idea. We **built it first**: 3 versions of a
designed advent book, about $1.50 of AI images and a full day of work, all based on
Google Trends alone. Then a 5-minute competitor check found a free, identical product
and a crowded marketplace. Verdict: NO-GO. It was archived before anything was spent on ads.

**Lesson:** Trends show that a category sells, not that *you* can win in it.
Check free alternatives, competitors and distribution **before** building anything.

Scorecard used at the time: demand 14/20 · competition 4/20 · distribution 3/20 ·
unit economics 8/15 · edge 4/15 · speed 9/10 → **42**.
