## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** **PIVOT**, 56/100. Brands do buy UGC. But a 20% broker cut has **no pricing power** against Billo ($99 per video with rights), JoinBrands (8–15% fees) and AI video at $4–11, and brands bypass the broker after the first deal.

**Key findings**
1. **Platforms undercut a 20% cut**: Billo sells a video with usage rights from **$99**. JoinBrands charges a **15%** fee on its free plan and 8% on its top plan. ([Billo, 2026-11](https://billo.app/blog/how-billo-pricing-works/), [JoinBrands, 2026-11](https://joinbrands.com/pricing/))
2. **Prices are falling fast**: UGC rates fell **44.37%** in 2025, as the share of creators offering UGC rose from 26% to 66%. ([Collabstr report via Net Influencer, 2025-02](https://www.netinfluencer.com/influencer-collab-costs-in-decline-but-ugc-offers-bright-spot-in-2025-report-finds/))
3. **Nothing locks the brand in**: Insense needs **12-month exclusivity** and voided refunds to stop brands and creators going around it. A solo broker has neither, so repeat orders leak away. ([Insense creator terms, 2026-05](https://insense.pro/terms-of-service-for-creators))

**Steelman:** Brands keep needing **fresh ad creatives** every 4–8 weeks, and TikTok Shop US grew 68% in 2025. The evidence confirms that demand. But the margin goes to whoever owns the brief and the results, **not the matchmaker**.

**Recommendation:** Do not launch a 20% matchmaking agency. Test a fixed $1,200/month ad-creative package for Shopify brands that spend $5–30K a month on Meta ads: send 100 cold emails in 7 days.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** cold-email 100 Shopify brands that run Meta ads, offering a $799 pilot for 4 videos. Stop if fewer than 3 positive replies come back in 7 days.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|-----|------|--------|
| 1 | Pick one vertical and list 100 Shopify brands running Meta ads (Meta Ad Library) | 100 brands with an active ad and a founder email |
| 2 | Recruit 3–5 creators from r/UGCcreators at a fixed $100–150 per video | 3 creators agree to the rate in writing |
| 2 | Put up a one-page offer: a $799 pilot for 4 ad-ready videos with hooks, paid through a Stripe link | Page and payment link live |
| 3–6 | Send 100 personalised cold emails and 30 LinkedIn messages | 3 or more positive replies |
| 7 | Ask every positive reply for a paid pilot | 1 or more paid deposit |

**Stop if:**
- fewer than 3 positive replies from 100 emails by day 7;
- no paid pilot by day 14;
- coordination takes more than 2 h per video on the first order;
- the first client rebooks a creator directly within 60 days. In that case, drop the commission model.

**Type checks (marketplace):**
- **Liquidity: hit.** Creators are oversupplied: 66% of them now offer UGC. Paying brands are the scarce side.

**Primary kill shot:** strong competitor. Billo's $99 price includes usage rights and sets the anchor brands compare against.

## 6. Market

- **Target customer:** small US DTC brands that run paid social ads.
- **Pain:** they need 3–5 new product videos a month to fight ad fatigue. Today they use Billo, Insense, JoinBrands, Collabstr, Fiverr, TikTok Shop free samples or AI tools.
- **Demand signals:** "ugc creator" gets about 135K searches ([Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/marketing-regular-topics-last-2-years?page=5), tertiary). Collabstr handled about 21,000 collaborations in 2025.
- **Trend:** volume is rising and the price per video is falling. Rates dropped 44.37% in 2025 (Collabstr, 2025).
