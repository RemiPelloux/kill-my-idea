# Cold outreach templates

Send from the secondary sending domains, never the main one. 100 emails + 30 LinkedIn messages in days 3-6 of sprint 0.
Log every batch: `python3 ops/tracker.py outreach --channel email --sent 20 --positive 0`.

Personalise the first line from the brand's live ad in the Meta Ad Library. No fake claims, no invented results.
