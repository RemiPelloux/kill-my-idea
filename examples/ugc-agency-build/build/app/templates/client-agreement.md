# Client agreement (template)

> Starting point only, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it before the first paid order.

Between **Hookline Studio** ("Studio") and **{{client_name}}** ("Client"), dated {{date}}.

1. **Service.**
   - Pilot: 4 ad-ready videos for **$799**, paid upfront.
   - Monthly package: 8 ad-ready videos a month for **$1,200 a month**, billed monthly in advance.
2. **Process.** The Studio sends a brief with 3 hook options per video. Nothing is filmed before the Client approves the brief in writing.
3. **Delivery.** Videos at 1080x1920 with captions, within 10 days of the creators receiving the product. Up to 2 revisions per video.
4. **Product.** The Client ships the product to the creators at its own cost.
5. **Usage rights.** The Client may use each video in paid and organic ads on {{platforms}} for {{months}} months from delivery. Longer or wider use is quoted separately.
6. **Claims.** The Client approves every product claim in the brief and is responsible for its truth.
7. **No direct hire.** For 12 months after the last order, the Client does not hire or commission a creator introduced by the Studio without the Studio.
8. **Notice.** The monthly package can be ended with 30 days' notice by email.
9. **Results recap.** If the Client shares ad results (spend, CTR, CPA), the Studio sends a monthly recap of which hooks won. The Studio does not promise ad results.

Signed: Studio ______  Client ______
