# Creator agreement (template)

> Starting point only, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it before the first paid order.

Between **Hookline Studio** ("Studio") and **{{creator_name}}** ("Creator"), dated {{date}}.

1. **Work.** For each order the Creator films the videos described in the Studio's approved brief, using the real product shipped to them.
2. **Fee.** A fixed **${{rate}} per video** (between $100 and $150), including up to 2 revisions. Paid within **7 days** of the client approving the video.
3. **Usage rights.** The Creator grants the Studio and its client a licence to use each video in paid and organic ads on {{platforms}} for **{{months}} months** from delivery, in {{territory}}. Any longer or wider use is agreed and paid separately.
4. **Honest content.** The Creator only says what is true for them about the product and never claims longer or different use than they had.
5. **Disclosure.** Where the video is posted from the Creator's own accounts, the Creator clearly discloses the paid relationship, as the US FTC Endorsement Guides require.
6. **Confidentiality and non-solicitation.** The Creator keeps the client's product and brief confidential and does not offer work directly to a Studio client for 12 months after the last order with that client.
7. **Product.** The product shipped stays with the Creator unless the brief says it must be returned.
8. **Ending.** Either party can end this agreement by email; orders already accepted are finished and paid.

Signed: Studio ______  Creator ______
