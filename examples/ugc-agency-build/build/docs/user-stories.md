# User stories: Hookline Studio

Generated by kill-my-idea:build on 2026-11-11 from the PIVOT 56/100 verdict, building rescue-1: Sell a fixed monthly creative package ($1,200 for 8 ad-ready videos with hooks), not a 20% commission, to Shopify brands spending $5-30K/month on Meta ads.

## Personas

- **Maya, DTC founder**: Founder of a US Shopify brand spending $5-30K a month on Meta ads, no creative team. Pain: Her ads fatigue every 4-8 weeks; she needs 3-5 new videos a month and has no time to brief and chase creators
- **Jordan, UGC creator**: US creator filming product videos at a fixed $100-150 per video. Pain: UGC rates fell 44% in 2025 and work is irregular; wants steady, clearly briefed orders paid on time
- **Sam, studio operator**: The founder running the studio solo, 15 hours a week, $1,000 budget. Pain: Every hour of coordination lowers the hourly rate; needs to know fast whether the model pays

## Must have

### US-01 · 2 pts · sprint 0

As **Maya, DTC founder**, I want a one-page offer that states the $799 pilot (4 videos with hooks) and the $1,200/month package, with a pay button, so that I can decide and pay without a call.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] The page shows both prices, the number of videos, revisions (2) and turnaround (10 days)
- [ ] The pilot button opens a Stripe Payment Link for $799 and a test payment succeeds

### US-02 · 3 pts · sprint 0

As **Sam, studio operator**, I want a list of 100 Shopify brands in one vertical running Meta ads, each with a founder email, so that the 7-day test has a real audience.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] 100 rows with brand, Meta Ad Library link and founder email
- [ ] Every brand has an ad active in the last 30 days

### US-03 · 2 pts · sprint 0

As **Jordan, UGC creator**, I want a fixed-rate agreement that states pay, usage rights and disclosure rules, so that I know what I am paid and how my video will be used.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] 3 creators accept a fixed rate between $100 and $150 per video in writing
- [ ] The agreement states licence scope and duration and an FTC disclosure clause

### US-04 · 3 pts · sprint 0

As **Sam, studio operator**, I want to send 100 personalised cold emails and 30 LinkedIn messages and log every reply, so that I can apply the day-7 kill rule on real numbers.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] tracker.py shows 100 emails and 30 LinkedIn messages sent
- [ ] tracker.py report prints positive replies and flags fewer than 3 as STOP

### US-05 · 3 pts · sprint 1

As **Maya, DTC founder**, I want a short creative brief per video with 3 hooks to approve before anything is filmed, so that the videos test angles I believe in.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] 4 briefs filled from the template, each with product, angle, 3 hooks and a shot list
- [ ] Maya approves the briefs in writing before the product ships to creators

### US-06 · 3 pts · sprint 1

As **Sam, studio operator**, I want to log hours per order and see hours per video and my effective hourly rate, so that I know if I hit the 2-hours-per-video kill criterion.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] tracker.py report prints hours per video and USD per hour for each order
- [ ] An order above 2 hours per video or under HOURLY_FLOOR_USD is flagged STOP
- [ ] Unit tests cover both flags

### US-07 · 5 pts · sprint 1

As **Maya, DTC founder**, I want 4 ad-ready vertical videos with captions within 10 days of the creators receiving the product, so that I can launch them in Ads Manager right away.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] 4 files at 1080x1920 with captions and the hook in the first 3 seconds
- [ ] Delivered within 10 days of product receipt, with up to 2 revisions done

### US-08 · 3 pts · sprint 2

As **Maya, DTC founder**, I want a proposal to continue at $1,200/month for 8 videos, sent right after the pilot, so that I keep a steady flow of new ads without re-briefing a new vendor.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] Proposal sent within 3 days of pilot delivery
- [ ] A $1,200/month Stripe subscription link works in test mode

### US-11 · 5 pts · sprint 3

As **Sam, studio operator**, I want a monthly production calendar for 2 package clients (16 videos), so that production stays inside 15 hours a week.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] 16 videos scheduled with brief, ship, film and delivery dates
- [ ] tracker.py shows 2 hours or less per video for the month


## Should have

### US-09 · 3 pts · sprint 2

As **Maya, DTC founder**, I want a monthly recap of which hooks won, from the numbers I share from Ads Manager, so that next month's videos build on what worked.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] The recap lists spend, CTR and CPA per video from client-shared numbers
- [ ] It names the 8 hooks for next month

### US-10 · 2 pts · sprint 2

As **Sam, studio operator**, I want a client agreement with a 30-day notice and a no-direct-hire clause for 12 months, so that a client cannot take my creators after the first order.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] The agreement states deliverables, revisions, 30-day notice and the no-direct-hire term
- [ ] Every paying client has signed it before production starts

### US-12 · 2 pts · sprint 3

As **Jordan, UGC creator**, I want to be paid within 7 days of the brand approving my video, so that I keep taking the studio's orders.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] tracker.py records approval and payment dates per video
- [ ] Every creator payment of the month is 7 days or less after approval


## Could have

### US-13 · 2 pts · sprint 3

As **Maya, DTC founder**, I want a short case study from the pilot results on the offer page, so that I can trust the studio before paying.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] The case study uses numbers the client approved in writing
- [ ] The page shows it with the client's permission
