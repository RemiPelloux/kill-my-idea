# Business plan: Hookline Studio

Generated by kill-my-idea:build on 2026-11-11 from the PIVOT 56/100 verdict, building rescue-1: Sell a fixed monthly creative package ($1,200 for 8 ad-ready videos with hooks), not a 20% commission, to Shopify brands spending $5-30K/month on Meta ads.

The model chose the inputs; `scripts/build-kit.py` did the math. Change an input in `plan.json` and rerun the script to update every number here.

## Inputs

| Input | Value | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| revenue per customer month | 1,200 | (estimate) The rescue path's fixed package: $1,200 a month for 8 ad-ready videos (verdict rescue 1) |
| variable cost per customer month | 835.1 | (estimate) 8 videos x $100 creator fixed rate (low end of the verdict's $100-150) = $800, plus card fee 2.9% x $1,200 + $0.30 = $35.10; product shipping paid by the brand; founder hours excluded |
| cac | 300 | (estimate) ($86 tools + 15 h outreach x $20/h) / about 1.3 clients per 100 emails; a $1,200 retainer closes less often than the verdict's $190 commission CAC |
| churn monthly pct | 8 | (estimate) Retainers lose 1.6% a month per the verdict's benchmark, but small brands and a new solo studio churn more: assumed 12-month lifetime |
| fixed costs month | 86 | verdict metric `fixed_costs_month` |
| start cash | 1,000 | (estimate) The founder's stated budget in idea.context: USD 1,000 |

Lifetime gross profit per customer: **USD 4,561** (LTV:CAC 15.2).
