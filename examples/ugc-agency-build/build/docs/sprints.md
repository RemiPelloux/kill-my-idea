# Sprints: Hookline Studio

Generated by kill-my-idea:build on 2026-11-11 from the PIVOT 56/100 verdict, building rescue-1: Sell a fixed monthly creative package ($1,200 for 8 ad-ready videos with hooks), not a 20% commission, to Shopify brands spending $5-30K/month on Meta ads.

Key metric: **Paid $799 pilots**, target 1 or more paid pilot by day 14 by 2026-11-25.

## Sprint 0: The 7-day test (1 week, 10 pts)

**Goal:** Run the verdict's 7-day test: 100 brands listed, 3 creators signed, offer page and payment link live, 100 cold emails sent, every positive reply asked for a paid pilot

**Exit (measured):** 3 or more positive replies from 100 emails by day 7 and 1 or more paid $799 deposit by day 14; otherwise stop

- [ ] US-01 (2 pts): a one-page offer that states the $799 pilot (4 videos with hooks) and the $1,200/month package, with a pay button
- [ ] US-02 (3 pts): a list of 100 Shopify brands in one vertical running Meta ads, each with a founder email
- [ ] US-03 (2 pts): a fixed-rate agreement that states pay, usage rights and disclosure rules
- [ ] US-04 (3 pts): to send 100 personalised cold emails and 30 LinkedIn messages and log every reply

## Sprint 1: Deliver the pilot (2 weeks, 11 pts)

**Goal:** Brief, film and deliver the first 4 videos while logging every hour

**Exit (measured):** 4 videos delivered within 10 days of product receipt, at 2 hours or less per video (8 hours total)

- [ ] US-05 (3 pts): a short creative brief per video with 3 hooks to approve before anything is filmed
- [ ] US-06 (3 pts): to log hours per order and see hours per video and my effective hourly rate
- [ ] US-07 (5 pts): 4 ad-ready vertical videos with captions within 10 days of the creators receiving the product

## Sprint 2: Convert to the package (2 weeks, 8 pts)

**Goal:** Turn the pilot into the $1,200/month package with a results recap and a signed agreement

**Exit (measured):** 1 client signed on the $1,200/month package and paid the first month

- [ ] US-08 (3 pts): a proposal to continue at $1,200/month for 8 videos, sent right after the pilot
- [ ] US-09 (3 pts): a monthly recap of which hooks won, from the numbers I share from Ads Manager
- [ ] US-10 (2 pts): a client agreement with a 30-day notice and a no-direct-hire clause for 12 months

## Sprint 3: Repeatable month (2 weeks, 9 pts)

**Goal:** Run a full month for 2 package clients inside 15 hours a week

**Exit (measured):** 2 active package clients, 16 videos scheduled, 2 hours or less per video and creators paid within 7 days

- [ ] US-11 (5 pts): a monthly production calendar for 2 package clients (16 videos)
- [ ] US-12 (2 pts): to be paid within 7 days of the brand approving my video
- [ ] US-13 (2 pts): a short case study from the pilot results on the offer page
