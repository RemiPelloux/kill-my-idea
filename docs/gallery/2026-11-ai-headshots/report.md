## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** **NO-GO**. Free Gemini already does the job, HeadshotPro sells the same $29 package with $10M+ in revenue, and with no audience a paid customer costs ~$286 against **$23** of margin.

**Key findings**
1. **Free does the whole job**: Gemini **Nano Banana** makes a professional headshot from one selfie for free, and TechRepublic publishes the prompts (2026-03). ([source](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-gemini-ai-portrait-prompts/))
2. **The leader sells your exact offer**: HeadshotPro charges **$29** for 30 headshots and reports **$10M+** revenue from 200,000+ shoots since 2023. ([source](https://www.headshotpro.com/about))
3. **Paid acquisition loses money per sale**: At **$5.71 CPC** and 2% conversion a customer costs ~$286 against ~$23 of one-off margin (estimate). ([source](https://seojuice.com/keyword-research/technology/AI-&-Machine-Learning/worldwide/))

**Steelman:** Demand is proven: HeadshotPro made **$10M+** and buyers save ~$275 versus a $304 photographer. A developer can ship in 2 weeks for under $100. Checked: true, but the leader owns SEO and affiliates, and free Gemini erodes the convenience gap.

**Recommendation:** Archive the consumer $29 offer. If you want this stack to earn, run the 7-day B2B team-headshot cold-email test first and stop at fewer than 5 replies.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.
