## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** **NO-GO.** A generic remote-jobs newsletter paid by sponsors earns about **$0.26 per reader**, but each paid reader costs $1.63. Free LinkedIn and Indeed alerts already do the job, and incumbents with 280k readers own the sponsors.

**Key findings**
1. **Each reader costs 6x what it earns**: beehiiv paid recommendations average **$1.63** per subscriber, while a reader is worth about $0.26 over 12.5 months ([beehiiv](https://beehiiv.com/features/boosts))
2. **Free alerts already do the job**: Working Nomads, LinkedIn and Indeed send **free** remote job alerts, daily or weekly ([Working Nomads](https://www.workingnomads.com/subscribe))
3. **Incumbents own the sponsor budget**: Remotive sells sponsors a list of **280,000+** readers, and Working Nomads charges $1,000 per issue ([Remotive](https://support.remotive.com/en/article/ycrtby))

**Steelman:** Interest in remote jobs is durable: remote work has held a **9–10%** share of job-seeker interest since 2022 (Indeed Hiring Lab). Sponsors already pay incumbents $1,000 an issue, so a list that reaches about 20k readers can earn. Against it, the incumbents took years to get there and had an edge this user lacks.

**Recommendation:** **Archive** the generic version. Spend 7 days testing the niche, employer-paid version before you write a single issue.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** a niche beehiiv page for remote HR and recruiting jobs, 3 community posts, and 20 employer emails offering a $99 featured listing.

| Day | Step | Verify |

| Day 2–3 | Post the list as value in 3 niche communities (no link spam) | at least 50 sign-ups |
| Day 4–5 | Email 20 hiring managers in the niche, offering a $99 featured listing in issue 1 | at least 2 paid or a written yes |
| Day 6 | Send issue 1 | open rate at or above 40% |
| Day 7 | Decide against the kill criteria | 100+ subscribers and 2+ paid listings, or stop |

**Stop if:** fewer than 100 subscribers by day 7 · 0 of 20 employers pre-pay a $99 listing by day 7 · open rate below 35% on issue 1.

## 6. Market

- **Target customer:** the readers are US job seekers who want fully remote roles; the payers are remote-hiring tools, job boards and career services.
- **Pain and today's fix:** remote roles are only about 9% of US postings ([Hiring Lab](https://d341ezm4iqaae0.cloudfront.net/hiringlaborg/2026/05/13171335/May-2026.pptx.pdf)), and job scams are common: task scams were nearly 40% of FTC job-scam reports in H1 2024 ([TechRadar](https://techradar.com/pro/security/fake-job-tasks-are-costing-victims-millions-ftc-warns)). Seekers use free alerts today, or pay FlexJobs $23.95 every 4 weeks.
- **Demand signals:** about 1.1M monthly Google searches for remote jobs, worldwide ([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/news/article/monthly-search-for-remote-jobs-on-google-stands-at-1-1-million-globally-report), secondary).
- **Trend:** flat. Seeker interest has held at 9–10% since 2022, and the remote share of postings is down from its 10.3% peak ([Hiring Lab](https://www.hiringlab.org/?p=18561)).
