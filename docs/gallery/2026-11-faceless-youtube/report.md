## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** **NO-GO**. With no edge, an AI history channel earns **nothing for 12+ months**. YouTube doubles the bar to 8,000 watch hours in February 2027 and demonetizes templated AI content channel-wide, and AI sleep-history channels already flood the niche.

**Key findings**
1. **The bar to earn doubles in February 2027.** From 2027-02-01, new YPP applicants need 1,000 subscribers and **8,000 watch hours** in 365 days, double today's 4,000 (Google, 2026). ([source](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/connect-communicate/new-opportunities-to-earn-and-changes-to-the-youtube-partner-program/))
2. **YouTube demonetizes this exact format.** Its policy bars "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production", and monetization "may be removed from your entire channel" (YouTube Help, 2025). ([source](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392))
3. **The niche is already flooded.** In 2025, 404 Media named **6 AI sleep-history channels**. One of them, Sleepless Historian, has 702K subscribers and posts about 4 videos a week. ([source](https://www.404media.co/ai-generated-boring-history-videos-are-flooding-youtube-and-drowning-out-real-history/))

**Steelman:** AI cuts a history video to a few hours and about $62/month in tools. The audience is huge (OverSimplified has 9.65M subscribers), and AI channels like Sleepless Historian reached 702K. The evidence cuts against it: those channels grew before the 2026 crackdown and the doubled bar, and earn about $1–3K/month at 702K subscribers (OutlierKit estimate).

**Recommendation:** Don't launch the AdSense channel. If you want to earn from AI video skills, spend 7 days testing whether existing history channels will pay for **AI visuals**.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

## 1. Could it be saved?

The AdSense model itself can't be saved for this founder: every ad-funded version stays below cost in year 1. The only path above 50 changes who pays.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test (rescue path 1)

**Cheapest test:** pitch 50 small history channels (10K–200K subscribers) a $150 AI-visuals pilot within 7 days. One paid pilot or more, or archive it.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|-----|------|--------|
| 1 | List 50 history channels with 10K–200K subscribers that post at least monthly | 50 channels with a contact email |
| 2–3 | Make a 60-second AI-visual sample for a real script from 3 of those channels | 3 samples done in 6 hours or less |
| 4–5 | Send 50 pitches offering a $150 one-video pilot | 50 sent |
| 7 | Count replies and paid pilots | 3+ positive replies and 1+ paid pilot, or stop |

**Stop if:** fewer than 3 positive replies to 50 pitches by day 7 → stop the service test. No paid pilot ($150 or more) by day 14 → archive the idea.
