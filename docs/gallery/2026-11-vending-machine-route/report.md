## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** NO-GO at 49. Three snack machines in offices and gyms net about **$137/month** on $9,900 of equipment: a **6-year payback** at under $4/hour of your time, in a shrinking office channel. Only a pre-signed, high-traffic site changes that.

**Key findings**
1. **31% margin before your time**: After 50% COGS, 10% commission, card fees and 4% shrink, a **$309/month** machine leaves about **$96**. ([source](https://thehustle.co/the-economics-of-vending-machines/))
2. **Offices half-empty, market shrinking**: Office occupancy was **54.2%** of pre-pandemic (Jan 2025) and US vending revenue fell **1.3%** in 2025. ([source](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/market-size/vending-machine-operators/1113/))
3. **$10,000 buys a 6-year payback**: 3 used machines at **$2,645** plus readers use the whole budget; the base case nets ~$137/month, so payback is **~72 months**. ([source](https://www.usedvending.com/i/Crane-National-167-Snack-Vending-Machine-for-Sale-in-Illinois-/IL-I-492C2))

**Steelman:** Snacks are a daily need, there is nothing to build, and a machine in a busy 24/7 site can gross **$400-1,000/month**; routes resell at ~1.16x revenue. The evidence supports that only for high-traffic sites, not typical offices and gyms (~$309).

**Recommendation:** Do not buy 3 machines. Spend 7 days pitching 40 warehouses and 24/7 sites; buy one used machine only after a signed placement with 75+ daily users.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** Before buying anything: pitch 40 high-traffic sites in 7 days and get 1 signed placement with 75+ daily users.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | List 60 high-traffic sites within 15 miles: warehouses, plants, 24/7 gyms, hospitals | 60 sites with estimated daily headcount |
| Day 2-4 | Walk in or call 40 of them; ask for the manager who owns the breakroom | 10 or more decision-maker conversations |
| Day 5 | Ask about headcount, shifts and current operator problems | 3 or more sites with 75+ daily people and no or poor vending |
| Day 6 | Get 2 used-machine quotes and a Nayax quote; model each site's monthly gross | At least 1 site projected at $400+/month |
| Day 7 | Ask the best site to sign a placement letter | 1 signed letter at 10% commission or less |

**Stop if:** 0 signed placements from 40 pitches by day 7 -> stop; Best site has fewer than 75 daily on-site people -> do not buy a machine; Projected gross under $400/month per machine at day 7 -> do not buy; First full month gross under $250 per machine -> sell the machine within 60 days
