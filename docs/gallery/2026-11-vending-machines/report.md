## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** NO-GO. Three snack machines at sites you still have to find net about **$200/month** on ~$7,800 of capex. That is a **31–39 month payback** before repairs, or roughly $6 an hour for your 8 hours a week.

**Key findings**
1. **Unit economics: ~$200/month for three machines.** At $250 gross per machine, ~50% COGS, a 10% commission and card fees leave ~$78 per machine per month, a payback of ~31 months (estimate). ([source](https://thehustle.co/we-interviewed-20-vending-machine-owners-heres-how-much-they-make))
2. **Big operators and smart coolers own the sites.** Operators over $10M take 65.2% of sales, and smart coolers are "displacing both vending machines and micro markets in key locations" (Vending Market Watch, 2025). ([source](https://www.vendingmarketwatch.com/home/article/55295667/technology-and-convenience-fuel-growth-2024-state-of-the-vending-and-micro-market-industry))
3. **Offices are half empty.** Kastle's 10-city office occupancy was 56.5% in late October 2026, so a 100-person office sells like a 56-person one. ([source](https://www.kastle.com/safety-wellness/getting-america-back-to-work/))

**Steelman:** Cashless is now 78% of vending sales and the industry reached $40.04B in 2025. A disciplined operator with 3 high-traffic sites can earn a few hundred semi-passive dollars a month. The evidence says this holds only at sites with 100+ on-site staff, and incumbents already serve those.

**Recommendation:** Do not buy machines. Spend 7 days pitching 30 sites with 100+ on-site staff, and buy only if 2 or more sign a placement letter.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** Pitch 30 sites with 100+ on-site staff in 7 days, before buying anything. You need 2+ written yeses.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|-----|------|--------|
| 1 | List 40 sites within 20 minutes that have 100+ on-site staff per shift | 40 sites listed with a headcount |
| 2–5 | Walk in or call 30 of them; offer free install, free service and a 10% commission | 30 pitches logged, ≥3 verbal yeses |
| 6 | Ask each yes for headcount per shift, their current operator and a signed placement letter | ≥2 signed letters |
| 7 | Price 3 refurbished machines and Nayax readers; model payback at $250 and $400 per machine | Payback under 24 months, or stop |

**Stop if:**
- fewer than 2 written yeses from 30 pitches by day 7;
- any signed site has fewer than 100 on-site staff per shift;
- the modelled payback is above 24 months.

**Type checks (local):**
- **location_traffic: hit.** At ~56% occupancy and ~1 vend per employee per week, an office needs ~90–100 badged staff to reach $50/week (Kastle, VendDiscuss).
- **permits_capex: clear.** Permits cost $0–500 and capex of ~$7,600–8,000 fits the budget.

## 6. Market

- **Target customer:** office managers and gym owners who host machines. The end buyers are their staff and members.
- **Pain, and how they solve it today:** staff want a snack on site. Today they get one from incumbent operators, micro-markets, smart coolers, free employer snacks or a nearby store.
- **Demand signals:** 81M US gym members in 2025 ([HFA via Athletic Business](https://www.athleticbusiness.com/facilities/fitness/article/15821794/hfa-report-reveals-81m-americans-were-fitness-club-members-in-2025)); cashless is 78% of vending sales and the average ticket is $2.01 ([Cantaloupe, 2026](https://www.cantaloupe.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Micropayment-Trends-Report-2026-Final.pdf)).
- **Trend:** the industry is at $40.04B (+18.3%), driven by smart coolers ([VMW, 2026](https://www.vendingmarketwatch.com/reports/article/55381943/state-of-the-industry-2026-smart-coolers-move-mainstream-as-revenue-tops-40-billion)). IBISWorld sees vending operators declining over 5 years (search snippet only).

### Ideal customer profile (ICP)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Facilities or office manager, or owner, of a site with 100+ on-site staff per shift and no current machine (warehouse, plant, hospital, 24/7 gym) |
| **Where to reach them** | Walk-ins at industrial parks; chamber of commerce events; LinkedIn facilities managers; gym owner groups; property managers |
| **Pays for today** | Nothing. Operators pay hosts a 10–25% commission; some offices buy snack delivery (SnackNation from $199) |
| **Budget** | USD 0: hosts expect a free install, free service and a commission |
| **Trigger** | The current machine is empty or broken, the incumbent leaves, a new site opens, or night-shift staff ask for food |

### Ways to use it

| # | Use case | Who pays | Effort |
|---|----------|----------|--------|

**Best option:** #3. It is the only site type where the volume covers the machine.
