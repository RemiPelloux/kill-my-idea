## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** **NO-GO.** Building it costs nothing, but **no channel** pays back. A $9 planner earns $9.2 per buyer, while a buyer from Etsy Ads costs about $15. It also competes with over 1M listings and with free full-year planners.

**Key findings**
1. **Every paid customer loses money**: Etsy Ads cost $0.10–$0.50 per click and convert 1–3%, so CAC is about **$15** against an LTV of **$9.2**, an LTV : CAC of 0.62 ([InsightAgent, 2026](https://insightagent.app/guides/etsy-ads-cost-alternatives)).
2. **A million listings, and page 1 is bought**: **1,044,640 listings** target "digital planner", and 55% of page 1 is paid placement ([Listify AI, 2026](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/digital-planner)).
3. **The market price is $3–6, or free**: comparable printables sell at **$2.79–$6.01** ([Etsy listing, 2026-11](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4463483569/momentum-planner-minimalist-daily-weekly)), and Passion Planner gives a full 2026 planner away ([Passion Planner](https://passionplanner.com/products/weekly-2026-planner-printable-pdf)).

**Steelman:** Etsy gives sellers access to **86.5M buyers** ([Etsy 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063726000019/etsy-20251231.htm)). The build cost is near zero, the purchase is impulsive, and some shops do earn steady money. The evidence: those shops won with years of reviews (the page-1 median is 6,400) or with their own audience. This user has neither.

**Recommendation:** Do not open a generic $9 planner shop. If you still want to try, test one $19 niche bundle with Pinterest traffic for 7 days, and stop at the first kill criterion you hit.

**Premortem:** It is 12 months later and the idea has failed. Most likely causes first.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** List one $19 niche bundle and post 20 Pinterest pins within 7 days. Stop if it gets under 150 listing views.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|-----|------|--------|
| 1 | Pick one niche and read 30 Etsy reviews of its top 5 listings, looking for unmet needs | 3 recurring complaints written down |
| 2–3 | Build one $19 bundle in Canva and 5 listings with niche keywords | 5 listings live |
| 4–6 | Post 20 fresh Pinterest pins that link to the listings | 20 pins live, 50+ outbound clicks |
| 7 | Read your Etsy stats | 150+ listing views and 10+ favorites, or stop |

**Stop if:** fewer than 150 listing views by day 7 · no sales after 500 listing views by day 30 · fewer than 3 sales by day 60 (archive the shop) · ROAS below 1.0 after $30 of Etsy Ads (turn ads off).

- **Pain** and how they solve it today: they want a layout that fits their routine. Today they use free PDFs, Canva templates or $3–$6 Etsy printables.
