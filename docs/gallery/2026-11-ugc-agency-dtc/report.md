## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** PIVOT. Matchmaking for a **20% commission** loses money. TikTok One matches brands and creators for free, Collabstr charges 10%, and each client costs more to win than they return (**LTV:CAC 0.9**). A fixed-price niche video package could work.

**Key findings**
1. **Matching is free elsewhere**: TikTok One lets brands browse and invite creators at **no platform fee**, and Collabstr charges 10% (5% on Premium) ([Hubfluence](https://www.hubfluence.io/blog/tiktok-creator-marketplace), [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/collabstr))
2. **Clients cost more than they return**: at a **3.43%** cold-email reply rate, CAC is ~$170 against ~$156 of lifetime contribution (estimate) ([Instantly](https://instantly.ai/blog/cold-email-reply-rate-benchmarks))
3. **Video prices are falling fast**: the average UGC price fell **44%** from 2024, and AI tools make an ad for about **$11** ([DesignRevision](https://designrevision.com/blog/ugc-creator-pricing.md), [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/en/blog/arcads-ai-pricing))

**Steelman:** Brands keep moving budget to UGC (Collabstr data, 21,000+ collaborations in 2025). The service needs no code and under $1,000, and small brands want someone to handle briefs, revisions and quality control. The evidence supports the demand, but not a 20% fee for the introduction alone.

**Recommendation:** Do not launch a commission matchmaking agency. This week, test a **flat $1,250 niche package** (5 videos, 3 hooks each) on Shopify skincare brands. Stop if 100 emails bring fewer than 3 positive replies.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

| # | Cause | Linked to | Likelihood | Early signal (first 30 days) |
|---|-------|-----------|------------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Brands found creators themselves on TikTok One or Collabstr and refused a 20% fee on top | free_alternative | H | More than half of replies ask why not hire the creator directly |
| 2 | Each client took more hours to win and serve than the commission paid | ltv_cac | H | Fewer than 2 replies per 100 emails, or more than 4 h per order |
| 3 | Clients rehired the same creators directly after 1–2 orders | costs_eat_margin | M | The first client does not reorder within 30 days |

## 1. Could it be saved?

| # | Change | Fixes | Score after | Difficulty | Effort | Cost | Requires |
|---|--------|-------|-------------|------------|--------|------|----------|
| 1 | Sell a fixed-price niche package instead of a commission: 5 ad-ready videos with 3 hooks each for Shopify skincare/supplement brands, $1,250 flat | pricing_power, ltv_cac: ~$500 gross per order instead of $154; editing and hooks set it apart from free matching | 62/100 (estimate) | doable | 1–2 weeks | under $200 | Basic video editing (CapCut), reading Meta ad results |
| 2 | Monthly creative-testing retainer: 8 creator videos plus hook variants for brands running Meta ads, $1,500/month | churn, ltv_cac: recurring revenue replaces one-off orders that end when the brand goes direct | 60/100 (estimate) | hard | 2–3 months | under $500 | Paid social performance skills you don't have yet |

Path 1 still keeps R1 (free matching, no edge), which is why it stops at 62. Paid social results for 2–3 brands would be the edge that lifts it further.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** pitch 100 Shopify skincare/supplement brands a $1,250 flat 5-video package, with a free hook teardown of one of their ads. Target: 3+ positive replies and 1 deposit in 7 days.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|-----|------|--------|
| 1 | Pick one niche (Shopify skincare) and list 100 brands running Meta ads | 100 brands with a named founder email |
| 2 | Write a one-page offer (5 videos, 3 hooks each, $1,250 flat); recruit 5 creators | 5 creators confirm rates under $150/video |
| 3–5 | Send 100 personalised emails, each with a 3-line hook teardown of the brand's current ad | 100 sent, under 5% bounce |
| 6 | Follow up once and offer a 15-minute call | 3+ positive replies |
| 7 | Ask interested brands for a $250 deposit to book a slot | 1+ deposit paid |

**Stop if:** fewer than 3 positive replies from 100 emails by day 7; 0 paid deposits ($250+) by day 21; delivering one order takes more than 6 hours of your time (raise the price or stop).

## 6. Market

- **Target customer:** US Shopify DTC brands under ~$200k/month revenue that run paid social.
- **Pain:** they need a steady flow of fresh creator videos for ads, and have no time to recruit and brief creators. Today they use Billo, Collabstr, TikTok One, Fiverr creators or AI tools.
- **Demand signals:** Collabstr's 2025 data covers 21,000+ collaborations and 200,000 creators ([Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/collabstr-report-tiktok-campaigns-plummet-as-brands-pivot-to-ugc/)).
- **Trend:** volume is rising, but the average price fell 44% from 2024 ([DesignRevision](https://designrevision.com/blog/ugc-creator-pricing.md), tertiary). No 5-year Google Trends series was found.

| CAC | ~$170 | estimate | (10 h × $25 + $40 tools) ÷ 1.7 clients per 500 emails |
| LTV | ~$156 | estimate | 3 × $52 contribution |
| LTV : CAC | **0.92** | estimate | $156 ÷ $170 |
| Payback (months) | 3.3 | estimate | $170 ÷ $52/month, longer than the 3-order lifetime |
| Fixed costs / month | ~$60 | estimate | domain, Workspace, cold-email tool, site builder |
| Break-even | 1.2 orders/month | estimate | $60 ÷ $52 |
| Time to first revenue | ~5 weeks | estimate | 1 week setup + 2–3 weeks outreach + 1 week delivery |
