## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** NO-GO. A generic pet dropshipping store on cold TikTok ads loses money on **every order**: break-even CPA is about $12.8 while new-store CPAs run $26–51, and Walmart already sells the same gadgets for **$9–22**.

**Key findings**
1. **Ads cost more than each order earns**: break-even CPA is **~$12.8**; TikTok's 2025 overall CPA is **$32.74** (Triple Whale via Influee, 2026), so LTV:CAC is 0.51 (estimate). ([source](https://influee.co/blog/tiktok-ads-benchmarks))
2. **Same gadgets cheaper at Walmart and Chewy**: self-cleaning slicker brush **$9.23** at Walmart, cat fountain $21.99 (2026-11). ([source](https://www.walmart.com/ip/16916129))
3. **Giants own the repeat pet buyer**: Chewy did **$12.6B** in FY2025 with **83.3%** from Autoship; Amazon holds 21.4% of US pet-care dollars (NIQ, 2025). ([source](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260325770300/en/Chewy-Announces-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Financial-Results))

**Steelman:** Pets are a **$158B**, growing US market and 25% of owners find products on TikTok (APPA), so a cheap store can reach buyers within a week. True, but the evidence shows reach is not the problem: **unit economics** are, at CPAs above per-order profit.

**Recommendation:** Do not open the store. If you want to stay in pets, spend 7 days and under $200 testing a $44.99 bundle via organic TikToks and $150 of ads, and stop if CPA exceeds $15.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** 7 days, under $200: one US-stocked product, a $44.99 bundle, 10 organic TikToks, then $150 of ads; stop if CPA > $15.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|-----|------|--------|
| 1 | Pick 1 product with a US-warehouse supplier and landed cost under $12; compute the CPA ceiling | Landed cost ≤ $12 and break-even CPA ≥ $15 on a $44.99 bundle |
| 2 | Shopify trial store, one product page, bundle at $44.99, real delivery times stated | Page live, 1 test order passes checkout |
| 3–5 | Post 10 organic TikToks demoing the product with a real pet | ≥ 5,000 total views and ≥ 1% link clicks |
| 5–7 | Spend $150 on TikTok ads ($50/day campaign floor) on the best organic video | ≥ 3 orders and CPA ≤ $15 |

**Stop if:** no US-warehouse supplier under $12 landed by day 2 · fewer than 5,000 views on 10 organic TikToks by day 5 · fewer than 3 orders or CPA above $15 after $150 of ads by day 7.
