# Fix: Notion kit for French real-estate agents

**The fixed idea:** a 59 EUR first-90-days Notion kit for new French mandataires. Network sponsors (parrains and team leaders) sell it to their recruits and keep 30% of each sale.

**Score:** 43 NO-GO → **60 PIVOT (projected)**. **GO (70) is out of reach.** This is a projection. `verdict.json` is unchanged.

## Where the points went

| Loss | Points |
|------|--------|
| R7 unprofitable model (Profit 2.0) | −10, capped at 49 |
| R6 no channel and LTV:CAC 0.57 | cap at 49 |
| ltv_cac 1.0 | −6.7 |
| pricing_power 1.5 | −5.8 |
| channel_readiness 1.5 | −3.5 |
| trend 2.0 / willingness_to_pay 2.0 | −3.0 each |

The biggest gain is lifting R7. That needs Profit potential above 2, and only the channel and price changes can get it there.

**Prerequisites**
- L1: 2 sponsors with 10+ recruits each (partner, **have: no**). A Lemon Squeezy shop with affiliate links, 5% + 0.50 USD per sale (have: yes).
- L2: interviews with 5 mandataires in their first year (data, 5 h, **have: no**). Notion relation skills (about 10 h, have: unknown).
- L3: one mandataire with 3+ years in a network who will co-author the kit for a 10–20% revenue share (partner, **have: no**).

**Recommended: 59 EUR one-off (match).**
- Kept per sale: 59 / 1.2 = 49.17 EUR before fees, minus 2.95 EUR (5%) and 0.47 EUR (0.50 USD) for Lemon Squeezy, leaving 45.75 EUR.
- Sponsor cut: 30% of 49.17 = 14.75 EUR.
- LTV:CAC 3.1 (estimate). This leaves out the founder's hours spent signing sponsors.

These prices come from search results and the verdict's research. The fetches this run did not show prices because the Gumroad pages load them with a script.

## Features

**Must have**
- **One-click duplicate.** The 19 EUR rival needs 20–40 minutes of manual setup ([Payhip](https://payhip.com/b/8zt52)).
- **Weekly pige planner.** The 89 EUR rival lists its planner as "à venir" (not built yet) ([Gumroad](https://lesheros.gumroad.com/l/lorganiseur-immobilier)).
- **Leads, visits and mandates linked to owner and buyer.** This is the core job.
- **First-90-days playbook and call scripts.** 75% of 2024 network recruits had never worked in real estate ([MySweetImmo](https://www.mysweetimmo.com/2025/05/05/immobilier-les-reseaux-de-mandataires-detiennent-desormais-27-du-marche-vincent-pavanello/)).

**Cut**
- A registre des mandats: it can't be sold as Hoguet-compliant.
- Portal listing sync: Notion can't do it.
- Belgian and Dutch versions: wait until 50 French sales.

**The one difference:** a sponsor view, where the parrain sees each recruit's weekly calls, estimates and mandates. Networks already run monthly coaching for recruits ([MySweetImmo](https://www.mysweetimmo.com/2026/07/04/conseiller-immobilier-independant-pourquoi-laccompagnement-fait-la-difference/)). It is easy to copy.

## Plan

1. **Week 1:** interview 5 first-year mandataires. Verify: 3 of 5 name follow-up or pige as a weekly pain.
2. **Week 2:** message 30 team leaders with the 30% offer. Verify: 2 sponsors with 10+ recruits each agree.
3. **Weeks 2–3:** put up a 59 EUR pre-sale page with affiliate links. Verify: 5 paid pre-orders in 14 days.
4. **Weeks 4–7:** build the kit with the co-author. Verify: 4 of 5 buyers log 10+ leads in their first week.
5. **Weeks 8–12:** add sponsors. Verify: 16 sales a month, which is 500 EUR net.

## Kill criteria
- Fewer than 2 sponsors agree by 2026-11-25 → archive.
- Fewer than 5 paid pre-orders at 59 EUR by 2026-12-09 → archive.
- Fewer than 10 sales a month by 2027-02-28 → stop building.

## Blockers to GO
- Notion's free CRM and 20+ paid templates hold Competitive room at 2.0.
- The number of mandataires fell 5% in 2024, and no search volume has been measured. Demand stays at 2.2.
- Networks bundle tools and coaching into 159–200 EUR/month packs, so agents have little reason to pay an outside seller.
- Founder fit: no real-estate know-how, no audience, and the goal is money only.
- Even a fully proven sponsor channel adds only 2.5 points. The ceiling is about 63.
