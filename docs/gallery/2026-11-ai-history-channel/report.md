## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** NO-GO. A new AI history channel earns **$0** until it clears 1,000 subscribers and **8,000 watch hours**. The niche is flooded with AI sleep-history channels, and YouTube is demonetizing that format. With 10 hours a week and no edge, year-one income is likely zero.

**Key findings**
1. **8,000 watch hours before the first dollar**: From **2027-02-01**, new channels need 1,000 subs and **8,000 watch hours**. Only 30.77% of channels that post 52+ videos a year reach even 1,000 subs ([MBW](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/new-youtube-creators-will-need-8000-watch-hours-to-start-earning-from-ads-double-the-current-bar), [vidIQ Research, 2026](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-uploads-1000-subscribers/))
2. **YouTube is demonetizing this exact format**: The **inauthentic content** rule (2025-07) targets mass-produced AI videos. Automated enforcement terminated about **130,000** templated channels ([Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/), [Fstoppers](https://fstoppers.com/artificial-intelligence/how-google-machine-terminated-130000-ai-slop-youtube-channels-six-months-903645))
3. **The AI sleep-history niche is already flooded**: 404 Media names a network of look-alike AI **boring-history** channels. Kapwing counts **278** AI-only channels among trending ones ([404 Media](https://www.404media.co/ai-generated-boring-history-videos-are-flooding-youtube-and-drowning-out-real-history/), [Kapwing, 2025](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/))

**Steelman:** History is evergreen, long videos carry mid-roll ads, AI voice costs about **$22/month**, and AI-only channels earn about **$117M/year** (Kapwing, 2025). But those winners got in before the 2025 crackdown and before the 2027 doubling of the YPP bar.

**Recommendation:** Do not launch the channel. This week, pitch AI-assisted history scripts or edits to 30 established US history channels, and stop unless 1 pays.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

## 6. Market

- **Target customer:** US viewers of long-form history videos. The paying customer is the advertiser, through YPP (55% of net watch-page ad revenue, [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en-GB)).
- **Pain and today:** They want calm, long history content for background or sleep. Today they watch free human channels and AI sleep-history channels.
- **Demand signals:** AI sleep-history videos draw large audiences ([404 Media](https://www.404media.co/ai-generated-boring-history-videos-are-flooding-youtube-and-drowning-out-real-history/)). Many AI history channels are monetised ([BOOM Live](https://www.boomlive.in/decode/ai-history-videos-india-reels-youtube-viral-29514)).
- **Trend:** unknown over 5 years (no Trends data pulled). Supply is growing faster than demand (estimate).
