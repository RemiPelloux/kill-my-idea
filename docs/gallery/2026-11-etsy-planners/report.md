## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** NO-GO. A generic $9 planner with no edge enters a market of **866,355 listings**. It competes with free 2026 PDFs and PLR clones, and paid ads cost about **$12 for every $7.70 sale**. Only a narrow niche with its own Pinterest channel gets into PIVOT range.

**Key findings**
1. **866,355 listings for 22,200 searches**: that is about 0.026 monthly searches per listing, and a new shop with no reviews starts at the back ([RankHero, 2026-10](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planners))
2. **Paid ads lose money on every sale**: a $0.30 CPC at 2.5% conversion is a $12 CAC, against $7.70 net per $9 order (estimate) ([InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-ads-click-rate-benchmarks))
3. **Free and PLR planners hold prices down**: free 2026 PDFs are available, 7,500-page PLR bundles let anyone clone a catalog, and the median price is $7.99, below your $9 ([Passion Planner](https://passionplanner.com/products/weekly-2026-planner-printable-pdf), [Etsy PLR listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4452251918))

**Steelman:** Etsy gives you 88M buyers ([Etsy Q3 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000090/q326shareholderletter.htm)), unit cost is near zero, and top shops have sold 50,000+ copies. That holds, but those shops started between 2016 and 2020, before 866k listings and the AI/PLR flood. The one 2023 winner found (PlannedSplendor, 17,257 sales) has a strong design brand, which you don't have.

**Recommendation:** Don't launch a generic $9 planner shop. If you want this category, run the 7-day niche test with $0 ad spend, and archive it if it misses the numbers.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** list 3 planners for one narrow niche with $0 ad spend, publish 15 Pins, and count views, favorites and sales over 7 days.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|-----|------|--------|
| 1 | Pick one niche keyword with under 5,000 listings and 500+ monthly searches | 1 keyword that meets both numbers |
| 2–3 | Design 3 original planners in Canva (no PLR) | 3 finished PDFs |
| 4 | List them on Etsy with $0 ads ($0.60 in fees) | 3 live listings indexed |
| 5 | Publish 15 Pins linking to the listings | 15 Pins live |
| 7 | Read Etsy stats | 300+ listing views and 10+ favorites, or stop |

**Stop if:**
- Fewer than 300 views by day 7.
- 0 sales from 10 listings by day 30.
- Fewer than 10 sales a month by day 90.
- Ad cost per order is above $7.70 after $30 of spend: turn ads off.

## 6. Market

- **Target customer:** US buyers of iPad/GoodNotes and printable planners (students, parents, people with ADHD).
- **Pain, and how they solve it today:** they want next year's planner. Today they buy one for $2–10 on Etsy or use a free PDF or template.
- **Demand signals:** 22,200 Etsy searches a month. r/GoodNotes has ~110k members and r/planners ~150k ([GummySearch](https://gummysearch.com/r/GoodNotes)).
- **Trend:** flat and seasonal. Searches peak at 40,500 in Dec–Jan and drop to 14,800 in spring ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-planners)). Etsy active buyers grew only 1% year on year, while sellers grew 6.5% ([Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-q3-2026-earnings/)).
