## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** **NO-GO.** Each TikTok-acquired customer costs about **$57** and brings $18 of lifetime profit. 30,000 Shopify pet stores sell the same products that Amazon Haul, Temu and TikTok Shop already list. With no edge, $1,500 buys 3–5 losing tests.

**Key findings**
1. **Every customer loses about $42**: CAC is $57 (CPC $0.80 ÷ 1.4% conversion) against $15.38 contribution per $34.99 order. TikTok CPM rose 16% in 2025 while ROAS fell 5.7% ([Influee, Triple Whale data, 2026](https://influee.co/blog/tiktok-ads-benchmarks))
2. **30,119 Shopify pet stores already exist**: 46.5% of them are in the US (Store Leads, 2025-03), all drawing on the same supplier catalogs ([source](https://storeleads.app/reports/shopify/category/Pets%20&%20Animals/Pet%20Food%20&%20Supplies))
3. **Pets has the weakest TikTok engagement**: Pets & Animals has the lowest TikTok CTR of any industry, 0.41% (Triple Whale), so each buyer costs more clicks ([source](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/tiktok-benchmarks))

**Steelman:** Pet spending is $158B and rising, TikTok Shop pet sales grew 60%+, and impulse buys need no sales cycle. The evidence says the demand is real. The profit goes to whoever owns the audience or the product, and this plan owns **neither**.

**Recommendation:** Don't open the store and don't spend the $1,500 on ads. Spend 7 days posting pet-product videos on TikTok for $0. Continue toward affiliate content only if a video passes 5,000 views.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

Neither path is "doable" in under 2 weeks. Both still face the saturated wave (R3), which keeps competitive room at 1.5.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** 7 days on a new pet TikTok account, 2 videos a day reviewing pet products, $0 spent. It proves whether you can earn attention before you pay for it.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|-----|------|--------|
| 1 | Open a new TikTok account in one niche (e.g. dog anxiety) and list 10 trending pet products from TikTok Shop | 10 products with 1,000+ sold each |
| 2–6 | Post 2 short videos a day reviewing or demonstrating those products, with your own footage only | 10 videos posted; at least 1 above 1,000 views |
| 7 | Review views, follows and saves, then decide | 300+ followers or one video above 5,000 views → continue; otherwise stop |

**Stop if:**
- Fewer than 300 followers and no video above 5,000 views by day 7 → stop.
- Fewer than 2,000 followers by day 30 → archive the idea.
- If you still test the store: a CPA above $15.38 after $150 of TikTok spend by day 5 → stop.
