Claude says your idea is great. This plugin tries to kill it.

kill-my-idea is a free Claude Code plugin. It researches your idea like a skeptical co-founder and gives you a sourced verdict: GO, PIVOT or NO-GO.

How it works

The most common use: paste a viral "I make $10k/month" thread. It checks every claim, names what the author sells and redoes the math. Or type kill my idea: … for your own idea.

It hunts for kill shots

A free alternative, direct competitors, people who already failed, no channel, costs that eat the margin, weak demand, legal risk, a saturated wave of look-alike launches. At least 20 sources, each tagged primary, secondary or tertiary.

It measures and scores

Market size, CAC, LTV, payback and break-even, country by country. Then 21 sub-scores against a fixed rubric, five rules that block a false GO, and a confidence level.

It shows a way forward

Rescue paths with their projected score and difficulty, and the cheapest test you can run in 7 days.

It fact-checks money tweets

/kill-my-idea:check-tweet checks every claim, redoes the math with real fees and names what the author is selling.

It gives you a card to share

Every report has a Share card button that downloads a PNG verdict for X, LinkedIn or Reddit. It's drawn locally; nothing is uploaded.

It checks its own math

A checker recomputes every score from its sub-scores, applies the rules and audits the sources before you see the verdict. Reports are hardened and come in English or French.
