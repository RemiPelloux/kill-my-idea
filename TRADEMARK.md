## You can, without asking

- Say that your project **uses**, **works with** or is **based on** kill-my-idea.
- Use the verdict badge (`![kill-my-idea: PIVOT 63/100](...)`) and the "Killed by kill-my-idea" line on reports the plugin generated.
- Write about, review, teach or compare kill-my-idea, and show the logo next to the project's name in that content.
- Keep the name in an **unmodified** fork that you use to contribute back.

## Ask first

- A modified fork you distribute must use **a different name and logo** (for example "idea-roaster, based on kill-my-idea").
