## 0. Executive summary

**Answer:** **PIVOT 60/100.** The team, money and ad pre-sales are real, but Quibi asks people to pay for minutes that **free apps** already fill; even at its own 7.5M target, revenue covers about half the $1B content bill.

**Key findings**
1. **Free apps already own the moment**: Analysts say Quibi's rivals are **free** socials; Snapchat alone has 218M daily users and costs $0. ([source](https://www.lightreading.com/video/quibi-faces-struggle-against-free-social-media/a/d-id/756820))
2. **Even the target loses money**: 7.5M subs x ~$6 + ads is ~$57M/month against **$83M/month** of content alone (estimate). ([source](https://tubefilter.com/2019/06/11/quibi-too-big-to-fail-katzenberg-mobile-video-billion-dollars/))
3. **The graveyard is full and recent**: Go90 lost an estimated **$658M** before closing in 2018; Vessel and YouTube paid originals also retreated. ([source](https://thewrap.com/before-quibi-mobile-video-fumbles))

**Steelman:** Phones passed TV in daily time, and Quibi brings Hollywood talent, $1.4B, a T-Mobile deal and **$150M** pre-sold ads: no mobile video entrant was ever this well equipped. The evidence backs execution, not willingness to pay.

**Recommendation:** Do not launch paid-only: add a **free ad tier** and halve the slate before 2020-04-06, then gate further content on 30-day retention.

**Premortem:** 12 months later, the idea failed. Most likely causes first.

## 2. Next step: 7-day test

**Cheapest test:** 7-day pricing fake door: card-on-file pre-orders at $4.99 / $7.99 from $200k of social ads to US 18-34; need >= 2% of visitors.

| Day | Step | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Launch a pricing fake door: quibi.com waitlist showing $4.99 / $7.99 with card-on-file pre-order, 3 trailers | Page live, tracking 2 price variants |
| Day 2 | Spend $200k on TikTok, Snapchat and Instagram ads at US 18-34, split by price | >= 2M impressions served |
| Day 4 | Release 2 full episodes free on YouTube and Snapchat; compare completion vs native shorts | Completion rate >= 50% |
| Day 7 | Count card-on-file pre-orders per 1,000 landing visitors and per price | >= 20 per 1,000 visitors (2%) |

**Stop if:** Fewer than 20 card-on-file pre-orders per 1,000 visitors (2%) by day 7 -> add a free tier before paid launch; Episode completion under 50% on free platforms by day 7 -> cut the slate by half; Fewer than 2M app downloads in the first 30 days after 2020-04-06 -> freeze new content commissioning

## 6. Market

- **Target customer:** US 18-34 smartphone users watching 40+ minutes of mobile video a day in in-between moments (commute, queues)
- **Pain** and today: Idle minutes on the phone; long-form TV doesn't fit them; Free YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat, Instagram; Netflix on the phone ~10% of the time
- **Demand signals:** 191M US mobile video viewers (eMarketer via MacTech, 2019); only 39% interested in paid mobile-first shows (Hub via TheWrap, 2019).
- **Trend:** rising; mobile time passed TV in 2019.
